{{/* Every post, newest first. Posts are told from pages by their date, not by section: Micro.blog puts posts in different places depending on how a blog was set up, but standing pages (About, Classes, the course pages…) never carry a date. Same rule as Dragon Gate. Usage: {{ $posts := partial "posts.html" . }} */}} {{- $out := slice -}} {{- range site.RegularPages -}} {{- if not .Date.IsZero -}} {{- $out = $out | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return (sort $out "Date" "desc") -}}
